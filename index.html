Questions

Good to know.

How much does a service call cost?

Our diagnostic visit is $129, and it's credited toward the repair when you approve the work the same visit.

Do you give the price before starting?

Yes. Your plumber inspects the job and gives you a flat-rate price. Nothing starts until you approve it.

I already bought my faucet or toilet. Can you install it?

Yes. Most of our fixture installs are customer-supplied. We can also haul away the old fixture.

Can you replace my water heater today?

On most standard gas and electric tank heaters, yes. Tankless and heat pump installs start with a free in-home estimate.

Do you pull permits?

When the job requires one, we pull the permit and meet the inspector. City permit fees are billed at cost.
